Philippine Tourist Spots

Welcome to the Philippines!

This webpage provides assistance for traveling to the Philippines, helping you find the places you wish to visit and discover what each location has to offer.

Luzon Tourist Spots

Luzon's tourist spots are known for blending Spanish colonial history, indigenous mountain heritage, active volcanoes, and coastal adventures across the Philippines' largest island.

Tourist Spots Activities/Things to Enjoy

Masungi Georeserve

 Masungi Georeserve offers exciting nature experiences, including ranger-guided treks through ancient limestone formations, hanging spiderweb viewpoints, rope bridges, caves, forest trails, tree-nurturing activities, and relaxing garden picnics.

Hundred Islands

 Hundred Islands offers fun activities such as visiting scenic islands and caves, riding ziplines, snorkeling, helmet diving, kayaking, paddleboarding, banana boat rides, cliff jumping, and scuba diving.

Intramuros

 Explore Spanish colonial fortifications like Fort Santiago, historic churches, and nearby Luneta landmarks, including the Rizal Park Musical Dancing Fountain.

Vigan

 Walk along preserved cobblestone streets and admire Spanish-era houses along Calle Crisologo.

Mt. Pinatubo

 Ride 4x4 vehicles along scenic trails to a volcanic crater lake and explore nearby coastal and heritage destinations.

Visayas Tourist Spots

Tourist spots in the Visayas are known for white-sand beaches, world-class diving and marine wildlife, unique geological and natural wonders, Spanish colonial heritage, and vibrant festivals.

Tourist Spots Activities/Things to Enjoy

Chocolate Hills

 Enjoy the beautiful blue river, famous for its exceptionally clear water and magical appearance.

Tinuy-an Falls

 Admire the stunning multi-layered waterfall surrounded by lush greenery and beautiful natural scenery.

Samal Island

 Relax on beautiful beaches, visit resorts, and enjoy breathtaking ocean views.

Magellan's Cross

 Learn about the arrival of Ferdinand Magellan in Cebu in 1521 and the introduction of Christianity to the Philippines.

Boracay Island

 Enjoy the beautiful white sandbar and breathtaking views of the nearby mountains.

Mindanao Tourist Spots

Mindanao tourist spots are best known for outdoor adventures, natural landscapes, diverse indigenous and Muslim cultures, and regional cuisine. As the second-largest island in the Philippines, it offers everything from high mountain peaks and waterfalls to white-sand beaches, surf breaks, and heritage landmarks.

Tourist Spots Activities/Things to Enjoy

Mt. Apo

 Hike the highest mountain in the Philippines and explore its rich biodiversity, beautiful landscapes, and mossy forests.

Hinatuan Enchanted River

 Visit the deep river famous for its exceptionally clear, blue-colored water and mysterious beauty.

Maria Cristina Falls

 Admire the famous Twin Falls, where the water divides into two streams around a large rock formation, and discover its importance as a source of hydroelectric power in Mindanao.

Camiguin Island

 Explore the Island Born of Fire, famous for its seven volcanoes, beautiful beaches, and stunning White Island sandbar.

Tinago Falls

 Discover the hidden waterfall nestled in a deep ravine, featuring a beautiful swimming lagoon accessible through a long staircase.
